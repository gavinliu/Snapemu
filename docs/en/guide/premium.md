# Premium

The core Snapemu experience is free. Premium adds extra personalization options so Snapemu fits your habits even better.

## Planned Benefits

- **Controller skins**: Change the look of the virtual controller.
- **More cover displays**: Use richer cover display options in your game library.
- **Home screen shortcuts (Android only)**: Add a game to your system home screen from its details page and jump right back in.

## Availability and Pricing

Some benefits are still in preparation and not yet available. Prices, platforms, and final plans will be confirmed on the Premium page in the app.
